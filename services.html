Our Services

Dirt Girls Garden Design services

We handle every stage of your landscape and porchscape transformation—from design and sourcing to installation and hardscaping. By prioritizing Florida-native plants and adaptable ornamentals, we create vibrant, low-maintenance yards and container spaces that thrive naturally and support local pollinators.

01 — CONSULTATION

Consultation

We start by understanding your property, your vision, and how you want to live in your outdoor space. Our consultation sets the foundation for a sustainable, easy-care landscape.

  • Design consultation with plant and material recommendations
  • Consultation for sustainable, easy-care outdoor spaces
  • Landscaping material recommendations — plants, rock, and features
  • Site assessment for sun, soil, drainage, and wildlife goals
Request a consultation

02 — DESIGN

Design

Personalized landscape design tailored to your vision, property, and lifestyle. We deliver detailed plans you can use to bring your native-plant garden to life.

  • Personalized landscape design for your property and lifestyle
  • DIY design packages with detailed plans and plant lists (starting at $150)
  • Florida-native plant design and low-maintenance, water-efficient layouts
  • Digital or printed design delivery
See design examples

03 — INSTALLATION

Installation

Complete landscape implementation from sourcing to planting. We handle every detail so your garden is installed with care and built to thrive.

  • Complete landscape implementation and installation
  • Softscaping, garden beds, and border installation
  • Tree selection and planting for shade, structure, and beauty
  • Premium mulch application for moisture retention and weed suppression
  • Plant sourcing and procurement
  • Eco-friendly, sustainable landscaping solutions
Request installation
